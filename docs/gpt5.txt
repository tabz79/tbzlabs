# The Hard Truth

The current version feels like:

"A beautifully designed SynOS case study."

What we are trying to build is:

"An interactive operating system museum."

Those are completely different products.

The visual foundation is already strong.

The structure is what needs to change.

Reduce text by 70%.

Increase interaction by 300%.

Make every section answer a question through interaction instead of explanation.

The network of connected operational nodes should become the hero of the entire experience.

The visitor should leave feeling they have operated a diagnostic center through SynOS, not read about one.
